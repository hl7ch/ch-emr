# Resolution Desk Ballot CH EMR STU 1

## General information
The STU1 ballot was held from August 3 to September 30. From September 22 to September 23, 
the annual Digital Projectathon took place, where there was the opportunity to test the 
CH EMR exchange format and provide feedback.   
During the ballot, 13 issues were reported for CH EMR:
* [Issues CH EMR](https://github.com/hl7ch/ch-emr/issues?q=label%3A%22STU+1+Ballot%22)

## Dependencies
* [Issue #42](https://github.com/hl7ch/ch-emr/issues/42): Question about dependencies
* [Issue #40](https://github.com/hl7ch/ch-emr/issues/40): Check dependencies – newer versions available for hl7.fhir.uv.extensions.r4 and hl7.fhir.uv.ips

## Documentation (profiles/texts/examples)
* [Issue #39](https://github.com/hl7ch/ch-emr/issues/39): Incorrect numbering of the titles

## Profiling
* [Issue #51](https://github.com/hl7ch/ch-emr/issues/51): UDI NamingSystems: should CH EMR publish NamingSystems with http://hl7.org/fhir/NamingSystem/ identifiers?
* [Issue #50](https://github.com/hl7ch/ch-emr/issues/50): Implant body site: could the CH EMR BodyStructure reference extension be dropped, or else replaced by the R5 cross-version extension with an example?
* [Issue #49](https://github.com/hl7ch/ch-emr/issues/49): Bundle profile: could the obligations on Patient.communication.language move to a CH EMR Patient profile, as IPS does for Patient?
* [Issue #48](https://github.com/hl7ch/ch-emr/issues/48): Care team section: the practitioner and practitionerRole slices can never match, because they have no target profile

## Terminology
